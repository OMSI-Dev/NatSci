
*Critical note for PCBs versioned at 2/2026: Ethernet ports for TOF 1-4, 5-8, and 9-10 have TX to TX, RX to RX. Phoenix connectors to sensor components must have physical wires TX/RX swapped to work with PCB boards. Pinouts below follow this assumption.

3. (Blue) RX/SDA to Teensy 4.1's TX Pin 0
4. (Green) TX/SCL to Teensy 4.1's RX Pin 1
